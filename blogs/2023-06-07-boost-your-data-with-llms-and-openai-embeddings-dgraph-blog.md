Dgraph stores data as you think about it: a network of interconnected pieces of information, a Graph! The recent advance of language models from OpenAI, provides us with interesting tools to make your data smarter, and automate tedious tasks such as data classification. In this post we will show you how to use word embeddings in Dgraph to make your data smarter and have automatic classification.
